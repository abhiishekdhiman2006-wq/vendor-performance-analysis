WITH SalesSummary AS (
  SELECT VendorNO, Brand,
         SUM(SalesDollars)  AS TotalSalesDollars,
         SUM(SalesQuantity) AS TotalSalesQuantity
  FROM sales
  GROUP BY VendorNO, Brand
)
SELECT ps.VendorName, ps.Description,
       ss.TotalSalesDollars - ps.TotalDollarsQuantity
         AS GrossProfit
FROM PurchaseSummary ps
LEFT JOIN SalesSummary ss ON ps.Brand = ss.Brand;

1. Data Pipeline and Vendor Summary Table

Python·SQL·SQLite·SQLAlchemy·pandas

The raw data was spread over four tables, so every question needed slow, repeated joins. I built a pipeline that loads the files once and stores a single pre-aggregated table that analysis and dashboards can read directly.

What I built

  • ingestion_db.py reads every CSV in a data folder into SQLite in chunks of 10,000 rows and logs the total time taken.
  • get_vendor_summary.py joins purchases, purchase prices, sales and freight invoices with three SQL CTEs.
  • Cleaning: filled missing values, fixed data types, trimmed text columns.
  • New columns: gross profit, profit margin, stock turnover and sales-to-purchase ratio.

Result

  • One table with 10,692 vendor and brand rows, saved as vendor_sales_summary.
  • Exploratory notebook traced a single vendor across all four tables to confirm how they relate and to design the query.
  • Repeated expensive joins are replaced by one stored table.

2. Vendor Performance Analysis

Python·pandas·seaborn·Matplotlib·SciPy

I analysed 8,564 qualifying rows (positive profit, positive margin, sales above zero) to answer six business questions for a beverage distributor.

65.69%of purchase dollars come from the top 10 vendors
72%lower unit cost on large orders than small ones
$2.71Mof capital locked in unsold inventory
198brands with low sales but high margin

Questions answered

  • Which brands need promotion or a price change?
  • Which vendors and brands sell the most?
  • How dependent is purchasing on the top vendors?
  • Does buying in bulk lower the unit price?
  • Which vendors hold slow-moving stock, and how much capital is tied up?
  • Do top and low vendors really differ in margin?

What I found

  • Diageo alone accounts for 16.3% of purchases.
  • Average unit price: $39.07 for small orders, $15.49 for medium, $10.78 for large.
  • Low-sales vendors earn a higher mean margin (41.57%) than top-sales vendors (31.18%).
  • A Welch t-test confirms the gap (t = −17.67, p < 0.001); the 95% intervals do not overlap.
  • Correlation of 0.999 between quantity bought and quantity sold shows stock mostly moves.
